Business context

The product is only part of the decision

Portfolio Studio keeps each data product connected to the business reason behind it. Objectives, signals, needs and use cases remain connected through portfolio review and into delivery. This gives decision-makers more context than a list of technical assets.

This connected context makes portfolios easier to review, explain, hand over and evolve.

Workflow as product

Treat the workflow around the portfolio as part of the product

Portfolio Studio does more than generate product definitions. It supports the workflow around them, from business material and portfolio generation through review, decisions, versioning and delivery handoff into tools such as Jira.

Delivery progress can be monitored from Studio so the original business context stays connected to execution.

Portfolio Assistant

Ask business questions with the full portfolio in context

The Assistant grounds its answers in your objectives, signals, use cases, products and decision history. Teams can explore priorities and rationale without reducing the experience to generic chat.

Answers remain transparent and traceable, helping teams reach aligned decisions faster.

Business to delivery

Move into delivery without losing business context

Approved portfolio items become Jira Epics and Features while the objectives, use cases and data product context stay connected.

Delivery status flows back into Studio, giving decision-makers a clear view from original intent to execution.

Open standards

Built on an open standards foundation

Portfolio Studio uses the Open Data Product Specification family developed under the Linux Foundation. This gives the generated portfolio a structured and portable foundation rather than locking its meaning inside the application.

Enterprise deployment

Designed for enterprise deployment

Portfolio Studio is intended for organizations that need portfolio decisions, data product context and delivery handoff to sit inside their own governance model.

The architecture direction supports controlled deployments and integration patterns without presenting every enterprise capability as already production-ready.

Customer environment

Portfolio Studio is deployed for the customer rather than offered as a public multi-tenant SaaS.

Business and technical governance

Keep decision context, data products and delivery information connected.

Integrated AI gateway

Studio includes an AI gateway layer for controlled model access, workload routing, guardrails, policies, budgeting and provider governance. Local AI-ready, with Qwen3 32B-compatible runtime recommended as the strong baseline for suitable evidence-handling and structured-output workloads. Frequently asked questions

What teams ask before getting started

What is Maysano Portfolio Studio?

Portfolio Studio turns existing business material into a structured data product portfolio for review, prioritization, approval and delivery.

What source material can we use?

Teams can work from the material they already have, including PowerPoint presentations, Word documents, Excel sheets, emails, PDFs, objectives, signals and use cases.

Does Portfolio Studio replace human review?

No. Studio prepares a structured first draft and keeps people in control of review, prioritization, approval and delivery decisions.

Do we need a frontier AI model?

No. Studio is designed for evidence-handling and structured-output workloads that can run with suitable local models or economical approved online models.

Can Portfolio Studio run in our own environment?

Yes. Portfolio Studio is designed for customer-managed deployment rather than public multi-tenant SaaS, so it can fit within your infrastructure and governance model.

How does Studio protect sensitive information?

Deployments can route AI work through the integrated gateway and apply PII masking, prompt-injection guardrails, controlled model access, workload-level policies and budgeting to sensitive source material.

Can portfolio items move into Jira?

Yes. Approved portfolio items can become Jira Epics and Features while their business context stays connected, with delivery progress visible back in Studio.

What does the first engagement include?

Depending on the selected plan, the engagement can include deployment, configuration, onboarding, administrator guidance and preparation of the first portfolio.
